Argentina – Great Diving Trip

1 Review
2 Days 1 Night Max 12 Guests Free WiFi Ages 18-65

Detail

Argentina's coastline holds some of South America's most spectacular dive sites. From encounters with elephant seals and penguins to exploring underwater kelp forests teeming with life, this is a diving adventure unlike any other. We cover the best dive sites, operators, and what to expect during your 2-day underwater journey through the crystal-clear waters of Patagonia.

Our experienced diving instructors will guide you through shallow and deep dives, ensuring safety while maximizing your encounter with incredible marine wildlife. Expect to see sea lions, colorful starfish, octopus, and an abundance of marine flora.

What To Expect

Upon arrival at the dive site, you will be greeted by your professional diving team. After a brief orientation and equipment check, you'll embark on your first dive in the sheltered bay. The second day features a deep-water excursion where you'll encounter larger marine life in the open ocean.

Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival & Shallow Dive

Check in at the coastal resort. Afternoon orientation session followed by a 45-minute shallow-water dive in the protected bay. Evening welcome dinner with the diving team.

Day 2
Deep Dive & Departure

Early morning boat trip to the deep-water dive site. Two dives with marine wildlife encounters. Lunch on the boat. Return to resort and departure transfer to the airport.

FAQ

No prior experience is required. Our instructors will provide complete training before each dive. However, basic swimming ability is required.

Bring swimwear, sunscreen, a towel, and a sense of adventure! All diving equipment is provided including wetsuits.

Participants must be between 18 and 65 years old. A medical certificate may be required for participants over 55.

Free cancellation up to 30 days before the tour. Cancellations within 15-30 days incur a 25% fee. No refunds within 15 days of departure.
